Privacy Policy

The short version: your patterns never leave your device.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What we store, and where

When you use the tracker, your patterns, round instructions, notes and progress are saved in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. A few display preferences, such as text size and sort order, are saved in your browser's local storage.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We don't have a server that stores patterns, and there are no user accounts.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • We don't upload, read or sell your patterns.
  • We don't use advertising trackers inside the tracker app.

Offline files

To make the site work offline, your browser keeps a copy of our pages, styles and scripts (a “service worker” cache). These are public website files, not your personal data.

Hosting

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. These logs never include your patterns.

Backups are in your hands

Because your data lives only on your device, clearing your browser data or uninstalling the browser will delete your patterns. Use Settings → Export all patterns in the tracker to download a backup file, and Import to restore it on any device.

Deleting your data

Delete single patterns from the Saved screen, or clear all site data for this website in your browser settings to remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
