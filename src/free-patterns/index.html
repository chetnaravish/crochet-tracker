Free Easy Crochet Patterns for Beginners

Three small, quick projects that use only basic stitches. Tap Open in Tracker on any pattern to load it into Crochet Tracker and follow it round by round.

Little Amigurumi Ball

  • Level: beginner
  • Hook: 3.5 mm
  • Yarn: worsted or DK, ~15 g
  • Size: about 6 cm
  • 12 rounds

The perfect first amigurumi. You'll practise the magic ring, increases and decreases, and end up with a squishy ball for a cat toy, a rattle or a pom-pom alternative. You also need toy stuffing and a yarn needle.

Instructions

  1. R16 sc in mr(6)
  2. R2inc x6(12)
  3. R3(sc, inc) x6(18)
  4. R4(2 sc, inc) x6(24)
  5. R5sc in each st around(24)
  6. R6sc in each st around(24)
  7. R7sc in each st around(24)
  8. R8sc in each st around(24)
  9. R9sc in each st around(24)
  10. R10(2 sc, dec) x6(18)
  11. R11(sc, dec) x6. Stuff the ball firmly now.(12)
  12. R12dec x6(6)
  13. FinishFasten off, leaving a 15 cm tail. Thread it through the front loops of the last 6 stitches, pull tight and weave in.

Want a bigger ball? Add one more increase round, e.g. (3 sc, inc) x6 for 30, and one more decrease round to match.

Simple Round Coaster

  • Level: beginner
  • Hook: 4 mm
  • Yarn: cotton, ~10 g
  • Size: about 10 cm
  • 5 rounds

A flat circle in double crochet that works up in about 20 minutes. Cotton yarn makes it absorbent and heat-friendly. Make a set in different colours.

Instructions

  1. R1ch 3 (counts as dc), 11 dc in mr, sl st to top of ch 3(12)
  2. R2ch 3, dc in same st, 2 dc in each st around, sl st to join(24)
  3. R3ch 3, 2 dc in next st, (dc, 2 dc in next st) x11, sl st to join(36)
  4. R4ch 3, dc in next st, 2 dc in next st, (dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st) x11, sl st to join(48)
  5. R5ch 1, sc in each st around, sl st to first sc. Fasten off and weave in ends.(48)

If your coaster ruffles, your stitches are a little loose. Try a 3.5 mm hook. If it cups upward, try a 4.5 mm hook.

Mini Flower Keychain

  • Level: beginner
  • Hook: 3 mm
  • Yarn: scraps in 2 colours
  • Size: about 6 cm
  • 4 rounds

A tiny six-petal flower that's perfect for using up leftover yarn. It uses chains, slip stitches and double crochet, and makes a sweet gift.

Instructions

  1. R1With the centre colour: 6 sc in mr, sl st to first sc(6)
  2. R2ch 1, inc x6, sl st to first sc. Fasten off the centre colour.(12)
  3. R3Join the petal colour in any st. (ch 2, 2 dc in next st, ch 2, sl st in next st) x6(6 petals)
  4. R4From the tip of any petal: ch 12, sl st back into the same st to make a loop. Fasten off, weave in ends and attach the keyring to the loop.

Make the petals pop by working round 3 in a brighter colour than the centre, or embroider a tiny smile on the centre.

How to follow these patterns in the tracker

  1. Tap Open in Tracker. The pattern is saved on your device and opens in Work Mode.
  2. Read the big, highlighted round and crochet it. Use the + button to count stitches if you like.
  3. Tap Done. The round turns green and the next one moves into place.
  4. Close the app any time. Your progress is saved, and you'll find the pattern under Saved.
