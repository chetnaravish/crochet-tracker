How to follow these patterns in the tracker

  1. 📲

    Tap Open in Tracker. The pattern is saved on your device and opens in Work Mode.

  2. 👀

    Read the big, highlighted round and crochet it. Use the + button to count stitches if you like.

  3. ✅

    Tap Done. The round turns green and the next one moves into place.

  4. 💾

    Close the app any time. Your progress is saved, and you'll find the pattern under Saved.
