Easy crochet for beginners

Which easy crochet pattern should you make first?

New to crochet? Work through the patterns in this order. Each one adds one or two new skills, so you learn step by step.

  1. 1

    Simple Round Coaster

    A flat circle in double crochet. You learn to start in a magic ring, increase evenly and join each round with a slip stitch.

  2. 2

    Little Amigurumi Ball

    Your first 3D shape in single crochet, worked in a spiral. You add decreases and learn to stuff and close a piece.

  3. 3

    Mini Flower Keychain

    A tiny two-colour flower. You change colour and make petals from chains and double crochet.

  4. 4

    Mini Crochet Sunflower

    Two pieces joined with treble crochet petals, so there is no sewing at the end.

Not sure what “sc” or “inc” means? Read how to read a crochet pattern or open the crochet abbreviations chart.

Design ideas

Free crochet design ideas with these patterns

One easy pattern can turn into many small projects. A few ideas to try:

Coaster gift set

Make four coasters in matching colours, tie them with ribbon and you have a quick handmade gift.

Bag charm

Hang the flower keychain on a backpack zip or tote bag. Try a different petal colour for each friend.

Sunflower clip or magnet

Glue the mini sunflower onto a hair clip, a fridge magnet or the end of a bookmark.

Ball garland

Make little balls in rainbow colours and thread them on a string for a soft garland.

In the tracker

Follow these patterns in the free crochet tracker app

Paper patterns are easy to lose your place in. Crochet Tracker is a free crochet app that works on Android, iPhone and computers, right in your browser.

  1. 📲

    Tap Open in Tracker. The pattern is saved on your device and opens in Work Mode.

  2. 👀

    Read the big, highlighted round and crochet it. Use the + button to count stitches if you like.

  3. ✅

    Tap Done. The round turns green and the next one moves into place.

  4. 💾

    Close the app any time. Your progress is saved, and you'll find the pattern under Saved.

Questions

Free crochet patterns: common questions

Can I download these crochet patterns as a free PDF?

Yes. Every pattern has a free PDF download, with no sign-up and no email. Tap Free PDF on a pattern (or use the PDF list above) to preview it, then tap Download PDF.

Is there a free crochet pattern book PDF?

Yes, you can read the free crochet pattern book online. It has all four beginner patterns with a contents page. The book is view-only, but every pattern in it can be downloaded on its own as a free PDF.

Do the patterns come with a diagram?

Yes. Each pattern has a round chart (a crochet diagram) drawn from the middle out, with a key for every symbol. The diagram is also in the PDF.

Are these crochet patterns easy enough for a complete beginner?

Yes. They use only basic stitches and small amounts of yarn. Start with the coaster, then the ball, the keychain and the sunflower. Each pattern links to a step-by-step lesson for every stitch it uses.

Are the patterns printable?

Yes. Print the PDF, or tap Print on a pattern page. The printout shows only the pattern, with a tick box next to every round.

Is there a free app to follow crochet patterns?

Yes. Crochet Tracker is a free crochet tracker app and row counter. Tap Open in Tracker on any pattern here, then tick off each round as you finish it. It works offline and needs no account.
