Where to start

Which stitch should I learn first?

Start with the chain, then single crochet. After that, pick the path for what you want to make.

  1. 1

    Chain and slip stitch

    Almost every pattern starts with a chain, and the slip stitch joins your rounds.

  2. 2

    Single crochet

    Single crochet is the stitch most others are built on. Practise until your stitches look even.

  3. 3

    For amigurumi: ring, increase, decrease

    Learn the magic ring, increase and decrease. With these five stitches you can make a whole toy.

  4. 4

    For blankets and squares: taller stitches

    Add half double and double crochet. They grow faster and make softer fabric.
