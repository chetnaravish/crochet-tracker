How to Crochet: Puff Stitch

A puff stitch is a soft, oval bump. You pull up several long loops in the same stitch and close them all together, like gathering a little bundle.

It is not the same as a bobble or a popcorn. A puff uses loops only, with no “pull through two” steps, so it looks smooth and cushy.

Abbreviation
puff
US name
Puff Stitch
UK name
Puff stitch
Height
About 2–3 chains tall
Chart symbol
Level
Easy

Step by step

  1. First long loop. Yarn over, insert the hook into the stitch, yarn over and pull up a loop. Lift it up to about the height of a half double crochet. ✓ 3 loops on your hook.
  2. Second long loop. Yarn over, insert into the same stitch and pull up another long loop, the same height. ✓ 5 loops on your hook.
  3. Third long loop. Do it once more in the same stitch. Some patterns ask for more loops, which makes a bigger puff. ✓ 7 loops on your hook, all the same height.
  4. Close the puff. Yarn over and pull through all 7 loops at once. Go slowly and turn the hook a little to help it through. ✓ 1 loop left.
  5. Lock it. Chain 1. This chain keeps the puff closed and is usually not counted as a stitch. ✓ A soft, round puff sits on top of the stitch.

In a chart

Crochet charts draw each stitch as a small symbol. Here is the puff stitch on top of a row of single crochet. Symbols can look a little different from one designer to the next, so check the key of your chart.

Where you'll use it

  • Puff stitch flowers and hearts
  • Soft baby blankets
  • Textured headbands and ear warmers
  • Bag and pillow fronts

Common mistakes

  • Loops of different heights. Hold the loops on the hook with your finger and pull each new loop to the same height.
  • Hook gets stuck when pulling through. Twist the hook so its throat faces down, and go slowly. A slightly bigger hook helps too.
  • Forgetting the ch 1. Without the locking chain the puff opens up. Always finish with ch 1 unless the pattern says otherwise.

Tip: Lift the loops up the hook as you go, so they stay long and loose. Tight loops make a flat puff.
