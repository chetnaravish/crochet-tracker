About Crochet Tracker

Crochet Tracker is a free tool for one simple problem: knowing which round you're on.

Every crocheter knows the feeling. You put your project down for a cup of tea, come back, and have no idea if you just finished round 7 or round 8. Paper notes get lost, clicker counters don't know what the round actually says, and scrolling a long pattern on a phone with one hand is frustrating.

So we built a tracker that holds the whole pattern and shows only what matters right now: the current round, in big letters, with a big Done button under your thumb.

What we care about

  • Easy with one hand. Large buttons at the bottom of the screen and a layout that works from small phones to tablets.
  • Private by design. No accounts, no tracking of your patterns, no servers storing your data. Everything stays on your device. Read our privacy page.
  • Works offline. Install it to your home screen and it keeps working without internet, so you can crochet anywhere.
  • Free. All features are free to use.
  • Helpful for beginners. Our stitch guides, abbreviation chart and pattern guides are written in plain English.

Feedback
