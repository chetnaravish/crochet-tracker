What Crochet Tracker does

It's a row counter that actually knows your pattern. Instead of counting taps on a clicker, you see exactly what to do in the round you're on.

✍️

Write patterns round by round

Every round gets its own card with the instructions and the stitch count. Quick chips insert sc, inc, dec, BLO and more with one tap.

📋

Paste a whole pattern

Copy a pattern from anywhere, paste it in, and lines like “R1”, “Rnd 2” or “R3-5” are split into separate rounds for you.

✅

Tick off rounds as you go

Work Mode shows the round you're on in big letters. Tap Done and the next round slides into place. Made a mistake? Undo.

🔢

Built-in stitch counter

Count stitches inside the current round with large + and − buttons. It resets automatically when you finish the round.

🎯

Focus Mode

Show only the current round, one at a time, so busy patterns don't distract you. Your screen stays awake while you work.

📴

Private and offline

No account and no cloud. Patterns are saved on your device, and the app keeps working without internet. Back up to a file anytime.

How it works

Three steps, and you're crocheting.

  1. Add your pattern

    Type it in round by round, or paste the full pattern text and let the tracker split it into rounds.

  2. Open Work Mode

    Your current round is shown big and bright. Upcoming rounds stay visible so you can read ahead.

  3. Tap Done after each round

    Progress saves instantly. Close the app, come back next week, and you'll be on the exact same round.

Frequently asked questions

Is Crochet Tracker really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, there are no ads inside the tracker, and you don't need to create an account.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Crochet Tracker runs in your web browser on any phone, tablet or computer. If you like, you can add it to your home screen and it will open like a normal app, even offline.

Where are my patterns saved?

Only on your own device, inside your browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Use Settings → Export to download a backup file you can keep or move to another device.

Can I paste a pattern I found online?

Yes. Choose “Paste full pattern” and paste the text. Lines that start with R1, Rnd 1, Round 1 or Row 1 become separate rounds, and ranges like R3-5 are expanded into rounds 3, 4 and 5. You'll see a preview before anything is saved.

Is it a row counter or a round counter?

Both. It works for patterns worked in the round, like amigurumi and coasters, and for patterns worked in rows, like scarves and blankets. Each step is simply a “round” card you tick off.

Will my screen turn off while I crochet?

Not in Work Mode. The tracker asks your browser to keep the screen awake while you follow a pattern. Most modern browsers support this. On older ones, your normal screen timeout applies.

Can I use UK crochet terms?

Yes. You type your pattern in your own words, so US or UK terms both work. If you're unsure which one a pattern uses, read our US vs UK crochet terms guide.
